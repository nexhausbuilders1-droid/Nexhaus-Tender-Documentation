Our Process

How a tender moves through our desk

A fixed six-stage sequence, because a real tender deadline leaves no room for improvisation.

1

Opportunity Review

We read the full tender document, eligibility criteria and evaluation matrix before accepting the engagement.

2

Document Collection

Your company profile, certificates, financials and technical evidence are gathered into a single tender file.

3

Drafting & Bid Writing

Technical and financial proposals are drafted to match the scoring rubric, clause by clause.

4

Compliance Review

A second reviewer audits the full package against the mandatory checklist before anything is finalised.

5

Packaging & Formatting

Documents are ordered, paginated, indexed and formatted exactly as the tender instructions require.

6

Submission & Follow-Up

We submit via portal or in person ahead of the deadline, then track clarifications through to the outcome.
