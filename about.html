Our Story

Fourteen years of watching strong bids lose on formatting, not merit

TenderPro Advisory was founded by former public procurement officers who spent years scoring tenders rather than writing them. What they saw again and again was contractors with genuinely strong technical capacity losing marks to missing signatures, disordered annexes and vague methodology statements.

We built the company around a single idea: if you understand exactly how a tender is scored, you can package, write and submit one that scores well. Today our desk supports contractors and suppliers across construction, ICT, healthcare, logistics and professional services, in both public and private procurement.
